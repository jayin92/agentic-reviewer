Paper Summary:
The paper proposes SparseSplat, a 3DGS variant that learns a per-Gaussian importance score for pruning and adds a view-dependent opacity regularizer, reporting 3.1x faster rendering at comparable PSNR on three standard benchmarks.

Paper Strengths:
1. Practically relevant goal with careful speed measurements (Table 3).
2. Full hyperparameters and promised code (Appendix B).

Paper Weaknesses:
1. The learned importance score is close to LightGaussian's significance score, which the paper describes inaccurately (§2, §3.1).
2. The 3DGS baseline PSNR in Table 1 is below the published number on the same split.
3. No ablation isolates the opacity regularizer.
4. Two recent pruning methods are missing from the comparison.
